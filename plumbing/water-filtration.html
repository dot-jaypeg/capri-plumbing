Orange County's water can carry hard minerals, chlorine taste, and sediment that affect everything from your skin and hair to your appliances and pipes. A whole-home filtration or softening system addresses it at the source, not just at one faucet.

Capri technicians assess your home's water and recommend a system sized to your household — then install and maintain it going forward.
