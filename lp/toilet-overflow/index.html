Overflowing toilet questions.

Turn the shut-off valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise until the water stops, and don't flush again. If the valve won't turn, lift the tank lid and raise the float to stop the fill, or shut off your home's main water. Then call Capri at (949) 594-0622.

Most overflows come from a clog in the toilet or its drain line. If it keeps happening, or other drains gurgle or back up at the same time, the blockage is often further down in the main sewer line. We find the cause before we fix it, and we can camera-inspect the line so you can see what's going on.
