Privacy Policy.

Last updated October 19, 2026

Capri Plumbing ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit a form, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email, city or ZIP code, and the service you need. We also record the page you submitted from and, if you arrived from an ad, standard campaign information such as UTM parameters or ad click IDs. When you call or text us, our phone provider records the number, time and length of the call or message.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and complete work, send quotes and invoices, and follow up about your job. We also use campaign information to understand which of our ads are working.

Calls and text messages

When you send us a request with your phone number, we may call or text you about that request, including scheduling, appointment reminders and job updates. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of purchase. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as the tools that receive our website forms and our customer management, phone and messaging providers, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies and similar tools, such as Google Analytics and Google Ads, to measure traffic and advertising performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to serve you, keep business records and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

California residents may ask us what personal information we hold about them, and ask us to correct or delete it. To make a request, call (949) 594-0622 or write to us at the address below. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
